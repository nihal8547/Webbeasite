# Local SEO configuration

## Google Business Profile

Keep the Business Profile verified and ensure the business name, primary category, services, phone number, website, hours and service area match the real business information.

## Service area

The public website currently uses a Doha, Qatar location signal and a Qatar-wide service-area signal without inventing a street address or map coordinates.

Only add additional cities or districts that Webbea genuinely serves.

## Reviews and prominence

Build genuine customer reviews, respond to reviews, and maintain consistent business information across relevant local and industry profiles. Do not create fake reviews, duplicate location pages, or doorway pages.
